Dosa

Dosa is a crisp, savory South Indian crepe made from fermented batter. Serve it hot for breakfast or a light meal.

prep time

15 min.

cook time

10 min.

serving

4 servings

instructions

step 1

Stir the dosa batter until smooth and season it to taste.

step 2

Pour a ladleful onto a hot, lightly oiled pan and spread it thinly.

step 3

Cook until crisp, fold, and serve warm.

ingredients

2 cups dosa batter

1 tbsp oil

Salt to taste

tools

Mixing bowl

Ladle

Flat frying pan
