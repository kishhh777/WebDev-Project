Vegetable Soup

A warm, comforting soup made with carrots, potatoes, and onion. Simmer it in vegetable broth for an easy, satisfying meal.

prep time

15 min.

cook time

30 min.

serving

6 servings

instructions

step 1

Wash and chop the vegetables into bite-sized pieces.

step 2

Add the vegetables and broth to a large pot and bring to a simmer.

step 3

Cook until tender, season to taste, and serve warm.

ingredients

4 cups vegetable broth

2 carrots

2 potatoes

1 onion

tools

Large pot

Cutting board

Knife
